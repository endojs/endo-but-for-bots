## Description

> * **module structure**: passStyleOf.js imports from helpers/remotable.js, and IIRC we try to avoid cycles.

> * **API alignment**: `getInterfaceOf` does not accept a `passStyleOf`/`passStyleOfRecur` argument, and it may be presumptuous to assume use of the one defined in passStyleOf.js.
