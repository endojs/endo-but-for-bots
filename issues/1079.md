## Description

Endo currently creates a graph of compartments out of a graph of Node.js packages. Each of those compartments receives the same powers, by endowment of globals or modules.

Our intention is to grow Endo support for LavaMoat trust-on-first-use policy files. This would allow Endo to grant fewer authorities to third-party packages, based on the needs they reveal through static analysis.

The gap between LavaMoat and Endo is mostly the distance between faithfully emulating CJS and ESM at the same time.
