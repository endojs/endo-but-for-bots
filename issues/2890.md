## Description

In the [CI workflow](https://github.com/endojs/endo/blob/master/.github/workflows/ci.yml), there's a step "Install Engines":

https://github.com/endojs/endo/blob/426dd916b245d8037d125a7619a6c45cf3b1eb0a/.github/workflows/ci.yml#L87-L88

This step fails randomly, presumably due to network hiccups.

Potential solutions:

- Rerun a failed job (example: see [this workflow](https://github.com/LavaMoat/LavaMoat/blob/main/.github/workflows/rerun.yml) and [associated job](https://github.com/LavaMoat/LavaMoat/blob/main/.github/workflows/nodejs-macos.yml#L25-L35))
- Remove this job from the `push` and `pull_request` events and have it run manually (pre-release) or on a schedule.
