## Summary

- **What:** Treats `--condition development` (the new `-C` flag from #2373) as the trigger for entraining `devDependencies` in `bundle-source`, replacing the explicit `dev` flag inside `@endo/compartment-mapper`.
- **Themes:** bundle-source, compartment-mapper, ecosystem-compat
- **Status:** merged

## Description

## Description

From `bundle-source`, a bundle cannot currently take on a dependency on any of the packages entrained by `devDependencies` in the `package.json` of the entry package. This change (indirectly) enables a bundle to capture dev dependencies by providing the flag `--condition development` from the [`-C` flag introduced in #2373](https://github.com/endojs/endo/pull/2373)
