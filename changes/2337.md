## Summary

- **What:** Calls `lockdown()` before bundling the `ses` shim itself so that any non-standard JS used after lockdown trips a `TypeError` at build time and pre-lockdown removals (e.g., "Removing unpermitted intrinsics") log during `yarn build`.
- **Why:** Self-applies SES to its own build to catch regressions in shim purity early.
- **Themes:** ses-lockdown, tooling, ci
- **Status:** merged
