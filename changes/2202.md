## Summary

- **What:** Adds `compartment.importNow` and an `ImportNowHook` to `@endo/ses` `Compartment` options; refactors the loader onto generators driven by sync- or async-trampolines so async and sync paths share code; adds a `COMPARTMENT_LOAD_ERRORS` env knob for verbose aggregated load-error stacks.
- **Why:** Refs #2201 — needed for Moddable/XS embedded targets, Module Harmony alignment, and Node.js `import.now`-style sync ESM-from-CJS; only invokes the hook when the moduleMap doesn't already have the specifier.
- **Themes:** ses-lockdown, compartments, ecosystem-compat
