## Summary

- **What:** Tweaks `safe-promise` symbol detection in `@endo/ses` to tolerate cases where `Promise.prototype` is unaffected (e.g. debugger attached after lockdown).
- **Why:** Author saw a throw under the vscode debugger; ultimately closed as misdiagnosed (see issue #1298).
- **Themes:** ses-lockdown, eventual-send, ecosystem-compat
- **Status:** closed-unmerged

## Description

A debugger may be activated after lockdown, in which case `Promise.prototype` may be unaffected, and therefore not a good source for the symbols we wish to tolerate.

I was getting a throw because of this when running code under the vscode debugger. After this fix, I was able to resume my debugging.
