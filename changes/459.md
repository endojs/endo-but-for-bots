### @warner — APPROVED — 2020-09-18T21:59:18Z

Yeah, seems reasonable, and marginally better. `execute` emphasizes the side-effects during load, which is the primary use case for what we'd call an "application", but kind of denies the existence of the return value. `import` emphasizes the namespace you get back, but also implies the side-effects because everyone knows that top-level code gets executed during an `import` statement. The latter is better.
