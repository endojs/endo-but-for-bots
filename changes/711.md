## Summary

- **What:** Stops `ses` from defining single `defineProperty` calls in terms of `defineProperties` to dodge WebKit bug 222538 (#592 follow-up).
- **Themes:** ses-lockdown, ecosystem-compat
- **Status:** merged

## Description

See https://bugs.webkit.org/show_bug.cgi?id=222538#c17

By no longer defining `defineProperty` in terms of `defineProperties`, we should avoid this infinite recursion bug. Because this should have no observable effect other than on a buggy Safari, there's nothing to test.
