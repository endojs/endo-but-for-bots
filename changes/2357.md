## Description

While investigating https://github.com/endojs/endo/issues/2354 , I just tried it locally by visiting 

    file:///.../endojs/endo/packages/ses/demos/console/index.html

and

    file:///.../endojs/endo/packages/ses/demos/challenge/index.html

in my browser. The first is the SES demo console, which worked just fine. The second is the SES Escape Room, which still relied on the disabled `Date.now()` not throwing. Indeed, before https://github.com/endojs/endo/pull/1718 a disabled (secure mode) `Date.now()` returned `NaN`. But https://github.com/endojs/endo/pull/1718 changed it to throw.

What's strange is that in https://github.com/endojs/endo/pull/1718 I revise endojs/endo/packages/ses/demos/challenge/index.html to adjust the text to say that `Date.now()` is "disabled" rather than "NaN". But I didn't fix the escape room code.

Although I found this while investigating https://github.com/endojs/endo/issues/2354 , this is a completely distinct bug that is unrelated to https://github.com/endojs/endo/issues/2354 . This PR itself does nothing to fix https://github.com/endojs/endo/issues/2354 .
