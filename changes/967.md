### @kriskowal (MEMBER) — 2021-12-28T00:36:14Z

I was inspired to coerce to Uint8Array because `t.deepEqual` didn’t think that the Uint8Array I put in was the same as the Buffer that came out. I can easily imagine using these adapters in situations that are equally sensitive, perhaps providing information about whether the program is running under Node.js or on the web. My preference is to present a stable type. There’s also the matter of needing to harden Buffer.prototype in addition to the Uint8Array prototype when this system is used in a hardened context.
