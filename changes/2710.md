### @gibson042 (MEMBER) — 2025-03-23T18:30:04Z

ECMAScript String and Array/%TypedArray% `includes` were introduced as fallback choices for what was [originally `contains`](https://web.archive.org/web/20141210155030/https://people.mozilla.org/~jorendorff/es6-draft.html#sec-string.prototype.contains), the [renaming](https://web.archive.org/web/20141212232513/https://people.mozilla.org/~jorendorff/es6-draft.html#sec-string.prototype.includes) (_and deviation from [Python](https://docs.python.org/3/reference/datamodel.html#object.__contains__) et al._) being forced by MooTools as recorded in November 2014 ([topic](https://github.com/tc39/notes/blob/main/meetings/2014-11/nov-18.md#44-arrayprototypecontains-breaks-mootools) and [conclusion](https://github.com/tc39/notes/blob/main/meetings/2014-11/nov-18.md#51--44-arrayprototypecontains-and-stringprototypecontains)). `String.prototype.includes` shipped in [2015 ES6](https://262.ecma-international.org/6.0/index.html#sec-string.prototype.includes) and `{Array,%TypedArray%}.prototype.includes` in [2016 ES7](https://262.ecma-international.org/7.0/index.html#sec-array.prototype.includes).

Map/Set/WeakMap/WeakSet `has(key)` was an initial method on the respective prototypes in 2015 ES6 (_followed by `Object.hasOwn` as a static analogue of `Object.prototype.hasOwnProperty` in https://github.com/tc39/ecma262/commit/077317b1529602407f25eab3e0e96fd5ae09c32d for the 2022 edition_). And Set-only `isSubsetOf`/`isSupersetOf` were added (_alongside other methods concerned with multiple elements_) by [Set Methods for JavaScript](https://github.com/tc39/proposal-set-methods) just last year.

----

But in Endo, a pattern function doesn't have the context of a prototype and so ought to indicate by name the domain of inputs for which it is applicable and corresponding predicate behavior over them—and they generally succeed at that! (_`splitArray` and `splitRecord` being the outliers as indicated by our own internal use._) This one is tricky because a) it generalizes across CopyArray/CopySet/CopyBag but not CopyRecord or CopyMap, b) it is limited to a single pattern in a way that a plausible successor won't be, and c) due to the wide open domain of its first parameter, future introduction of a successor would almost certainly take the form of a new method rather than domain expansion. And in the world anticipated by point c, the name for the single-pattern method should not suggest **behavior** of the multi-pattern method, which is my biggest objection to an unqualified name here... even `M.containerHas` seems like it might be the method to reach for if one has a subset or subbag constraint and knows that such a method exists, so the latter would need a name that does an even better job of suggesting its general multi-pattern nature (which is made difficult by point a already generalizing from sets to bags, stretching otherwise obvious use of "superset" for such a purpose).
