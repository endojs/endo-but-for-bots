Agreed, so LGTM.

However, I did look and ava;s `TestFn` is not a perfect fit either, as it says that a testFn must have all the enumerated TestFn-like properties. However, rather than stating a function-type-only for what these TestFn-like properties have in common, they define an entire new type for each one. Their type definitions are not DRY, and none of them perfectly fit what you're trying to say here.

However, `TestFn` is close enough, and is the best you can do importing one of their types. Even with the mismatch, I agree it is better to do that than to define our own function type.

No change suggested re `TestFn`.
