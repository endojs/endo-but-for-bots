## Summary

- **What:** In `@endo/pass-style` and `@endo/patterns`, makes `Passable` a generic type, adds overloads to `passStyleOf` so it returns the precise style, and redefines `Key` in terms of `Passable` (closing #1488, second attempt of #1933).
- **Why:** The previous `any`-based `Passable` defeated type checking; tightening it surfaces real downstream errors and improves documentation through types.
