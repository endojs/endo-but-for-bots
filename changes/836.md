## Summary

- **What:** Renames internal helpers in `ses`'s `whitelist-intrinsics.js` to use "visit" / "allowed" terminology and removes a `continue` flagged by lint.
- **Why:** Audit-sestival readability pass; "whitelist" was an awkward verb for the recursive culling routine.
- **Themes:** ses-lockdown, chore
- **Status:** merged

## Description

At the Agoric/MetaMask Red Team security review, we found in particular that `visit` might be a better verb than `whitelist` for the internal functions of `whitelistIntrinsics`. In this change, I went on to clarify the verb “whitelisted” with “allowed” and factored away a `continue` that eslint forbad for clarity.

I remain a little dissatisfied with the side-effective `isAllowed*` functions, but don't have a better idea since they both co-visit the property and permit and influence control-flow with both throwing an error and returning a boolean.
