## Summary

- **What:** Restores `String.prototype.toLocaleLowerCase`/etc. by aliasing them to their non-locale counterparts and provides a non-locale `localeCompare` shim, replacing #372's outright deletion.
- **Why:** Closes #335 and #378; downstream code (incl. agoric-sdk#1290) called `*Locale*` methods that lockdown had removed.
- **Themes:** ses-lockdown, ecosystem-compat
- **Status:** merged

## Description

Rather than removing the `*Locale*` methods, replace them with their non-locale equivalents.

`String.prototype.localeCompare` doesn't seem to have a builtin non-locale equivalent, so we provide one.

Fixes #335 
Fixes #378 
See #379 
See https://github.com/Agoric/agoric-sdk/issues/1290
