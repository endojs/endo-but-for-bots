## Summary

- **What:** Introduces `makeSecureBundle` and `makeSecureBundleFromArchive` in `@endo/compartment-mapper` that produce SES-wrapped bundles runnable without `eval`, using a precompiled functor and exported SES utilities (#1454, #1461).
- **Why:** Browser-extension and other CSP-restricted hosts need bundles that load without invoking `Function`/`eval`.
- **Themes:** compartment-mapper, bundle-source, ses-lockdown, security
